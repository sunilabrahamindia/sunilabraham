This record published by the [Centre for Internet and Society](/cis/) documents [Elonnai Hickok](/elonnai/) attending a learning session organised by the [Global Network Initiative](https://globalnetworkinitiative.org/) (GNI) in Washington, D.C., in March 2016. The session brought together the GNI and its new observers from the Telecommunications Industry Dialogue to discuss human rights impact assessments, corporate human rights due diligence and good practices in the information and communications technology (ICT) sector. ## Event Record

The GNI welcomed its new observers from the Telecommunications Industry Dialogue by holding a learning session in conjunction with the GNI Board Meeting on 10 March 2016. The session aimed to increase understanding between the GNI and the Industry Dialogue by examining common challenges facing ICT companies in the area of human rights due diligence and highlighting good practices. A second objective was to help the GNI develop a learning programme and materials that would be useful for its members and draw on their expertise. The learning session also informed the review of the GNI Implementation Guidelines that was to take place during 2016.

The session took place according to the Chatham House Rule. Each short presentation was followed by a question-and-answer session.

### Presentations

- Human Rights Impact Assessments in the ICT sector – Michael Samway
- The Human Rights Due Diligence Process at Nokia – Laura Okkonen
- Yahoo's approach to Human Rights Impact Assessments – Nicole Karlebach and Katie Shay
- Orange's challenges and approach to doing business in Africa – Yves Nissim
- Microsoft's human rights impacts and the warrant case – Steve Crown and Bernard Shen
- TeliaSonera's approach to withdrawing from Eurasia – Patrik Hiselius
- Considerations for company due diligence on the ground – Kathleen Reen and Babette Ngene, Internews

### Questions for Discussion

- What are some of the common challenges facing current GNI member companies and Industry Dialogue member companies?
- What do we consider to be good practices that are applicable to all?
- What lessons can be applied to the review of the GNI Implementation Guidelines that was to take place during 2016?

## Context and Background

The Global Network Initiative is a multi-stakeholder initiative concerned with the protection and advancement of freedom of expression and privacy in the information and communications technology sector. The Telecommunications Industry Dialogue brought telecommunications companies into a parallel industry-led process focused on responsible business conduct and human rights. The learning session documented here was organised to build understanding between the two groups and to share approaches to human rights due diligence.

The agenda brought together presentations on company-level human rights impact assessments and due diligence, as well as practical challenges arising from operating in different jurisdictions. The topics included Nokia's due diligence process, Yahoo's approach to impact assessments, Orange's work in Africa, Microsoft's engagement with a warrant case and TeliaSonera's withdrawal from Eurasia. The CIS record states that the meeting followed the Chatham House Rule; this archive therefore preserves the published agenda and discussion questions without attributing any additional remarks or interventions to participants.
