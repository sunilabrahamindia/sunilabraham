***A Radical Theology for the Present: The New Materialist Turn*** is a 2026 book by [Y. T. Vinayaraj](/vinayaraj/), published by Omega Book World. It examines New Materialism in relation to theology, Continental philosophy and postcolonial theory.

## Overview

The book discusses the agency of the material world and presents planetary humanism as a relational approach to connections among humans, nonhuman life and the wider material world. These relationships are considered in connection with power, justice and responsibility.

It also challenges colonial and imperial practices of "othering" and considers planetary humanism in relation to relationality and multiplicity.
