***Re-visiting the Other: Discourses on Postmodern Theology*** is a book by [Y. T. Vinayaraj](/vinayaraj/), published by Christava Sahitya Samithi (CSS) in Thiruvalla. The book is listed in Vinayaraj's bibliography as a 2010 publication.

The book's title is also cited in academic writing on Dalit theology and postmodern thought. One related essay by Vinayaraj, "Re-visiting the Other: Towards a Postmodern Understanding of Christian Mission", discusses how Christian mission has represented people as the "Other" and considers the effects of colonial modernity on those representations.

## Themes

In the related essay, Vinayaraj questions missionary approaches shaped by colonial assumptions that cast people outside Europe as "unreached", "uncivilised" or in need of development. He argues for recognising the agency of the people described as the "Other", including their ability to shape their own identities and social worlds.

This discussion is documented in Vinayaraj's essay on Christian mission. It is included here as related context; it should not be treated as a verified table of contents or a full summary of every chapter in the book.
