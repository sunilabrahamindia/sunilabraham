***Reclaiming Manyness: Re-reading M.M. Thomas in the Light of Indian Christian Theologies*** is an edited collection on the thought of M. M. Thomas in the context of Indian Christian theologies. Edited by P. G. George and Y. T. Vinayaraj, it was published by the Department of Research, South Asia Theological Research Institute (SATHRI), in November 2015 and runs to 208 pages. The volume is associated with a national colloquium held at Union Biblical Seminary, Pune, in August 2015. A documented chapter on Kuvempu, Thomas and Sarvodaya offers one example of the subjects explored in the collection.

## Background and themes

The Oriental Theological Seminary library catalogue records the volume as arising from a national colloquium organised by the Department of Research at the Senate of Serampore College (University). The colloquium took place at Union Biblical Seminary in Pune from 11 to 13 August 2015.

A chapter listed in Gladson Jathanna's curriculum vitae is titled "Kuvempu and M.M. Thomas: Sahrdaya Voices Propagating the Message of Sarvodaya" and occupies pages 153-166. Its title indicates a discussion bringing Thomas into conversation with Kuvempu and the idea of Sarvodaya. This is a documented example of a contribution to the volume, rather than a complete account of the book's overall argument.
