***Church and Empire: Detailing Theological Musings*** is a 2019 book by [Y. T. Vinayaraj](/vinayaraj/), published by Christian World Imprints in New Delhi. The volume brings together papers on the church and faith communities in the context of empire.

## Background

The book addresses the life and witness of faith communities in the context of empire. Its publisher's description discusses ecclesiology, ecumenism, mission and ministry, and the role of theological education in responding to contemporary challenges facing the church.

## Themes

The publisher's description highlights a critique of ecumenism organised around an imperial idea of "oneness". It presents the book as advocating a contextual approach to ecclesiology and ecumenism, with attention to excluded and marginalised voices. The description also identifies biblical interpretation, liturgy, ministry and theological education as areas through which churches and theological institutions can respond to these concerns.
