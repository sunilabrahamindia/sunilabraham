***Intercessions: Theology, Liturgy, and Politics*** is a 2015 book by [Y. T. Vinayaraj](/vinayaraj/), published by the Indian Society for Promoting Christian Knowledge (ISPCK) in New Delhi. The book brings together essays on theology and social and political thought, with attention to the Indian context and to experiences of marginalisation and exclusion.

## Overview

The book's stated aim is to bring socio-political theories and theologies into conversation, including approaches associated with Continental, post-Continental and postcolonial thought. The publisher's description presents this as an effort to explore ways of doing political theology in contemporary contexts marked by marginalisation and exclusion.

References to the book in Vinayaraj's other writing identify discussions of ecumenism and marginality, and of mission in the context of the margins. These references provide examples of topics addressed in the volume; they do not establish a complete table of contents.
