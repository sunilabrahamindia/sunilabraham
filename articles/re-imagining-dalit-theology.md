***Re-imagining Dalit Theology: Postmodern Readings*** is a book by Indian theologian Y. T. Vinayaraj, published by Christava Sahitya Samithi (CSS) in Thiruvalla in 2008. The title and publication details are recorded in Vinayaraj's curriculum vitae and cited in academic research on Dalit theology.

The book considers Dalit theology in relation to postmodern thought. In a discussion citing the book, the doctoral thesis *Towards a Subaltern Public Theology for India* refers to Vinayaraj's treatment of subalternity and the ways dominant-caste structures have treated marginalised communities as recipients of knowledge imposed from above.
