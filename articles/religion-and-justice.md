***Religion and Justice*** (2022) is an edited volume by [Rev. Dr. Y. T. Vinayaraj](/vinayaraj/), published by the [Christian Institute for the Study of Religion and Society (CISRS)](/articles/cisrs/) in Bengaluru. The volume brings together theological and interdisciplinary reflections on the relationship between religion and justice, including questions concerning religion in public life, ethical practice and social transformation.

## Background

*Religion and Justice* was published in 2022 by the Christian Institute for the Study of Religion and Society (CISRS), Bengaluru, and edited by Y. T. Vinayaraj. CISRS lists the volume among its publications as a 2022 book of 202 pages.

The volume forms part of Vinayaraj's wider engagement with theology, religion, politics, social justice and public life. His curriculum vitae lists *Religion and Justice* among his books as "Religion and Justice, (Bangalore: CISRS, 2022)."

## Themes

The volume examines the relationship between religion and justice from theological and ethical perspectives. It considers the role of religion in public life and the relationship between religious practice and questions of justice.

The book is situated within wider discussions of religion, society, politics and social transformation.
