***Re-imagining Reformation: Essays in Honor of P. G. George*** is a collection of essays published by ISPCK in association with the Council of Churches in Asia (CCA). The volume honours Rev. Dr P. G. George, identified by the publisher as Dean of Research at the Senate of Serampore University. It brings together essays on the theme of re-imagining the Reformation, including contributions connected with a national colloquium held in Madurai in August 2016.

Y. T. Vinayaraj's curriculum vitae records the volume and identifies his contribution, "Envisaging the Messianic Time: The Agenda of a Reforming Church".

## Background

According to the publisher, some of the essays were presented at the SATHRI National Colloquium held at Tamil Nadu Theological College, Madurai, from 8 to 11 August 2016. The event took place in the context of the 500th anniversary of the Reformation. The colloquium considered the church's commitment to reformation and renewal in India, and the collected essays were published in honour of P. G. George.
