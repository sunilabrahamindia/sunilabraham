***Theology after Spivak*** is a 2016 book by [Y. T. Vinayaraj](/vinayaraj/), published in New Delhi. It examines how engagement with the work of Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak might contribute to Indian theological discourse, particularly debates about the subaltern and the limits of established theological approaches.

## Overview

A bookseller's description says the book seeks to bring Spivak's thought into Indian theology and to reconsider how the subaltern is represented. It frames this engagement as a way to explore a theological approach beyond the limits the author identifies in conventional liberation theology. This summary follows the available catalogue description; a complete table of contents was not located.
