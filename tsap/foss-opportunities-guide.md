This guide brings together a small selection of sources relevant to work in free and open-source software (FOSS), open knowledge, Internet freedom and public-interest technology. It prioritises specialist sources and organisations whose work is closely connected to these fields, rather than general-purpose job-search advice.

The links are starting points, not confirmation that a vacancy is open. Check the original announcement for current status, deadline, eligibility, location, compensation and application requirements. A job, fellowship, mentorship, internship, grant and volunteer assignment are different kinds of opportunity; read the terms carefully.

## FOSS and open-source work {#foss}

These are the most directly relevant starting points for people looking for work connected to open-source software, open technology or open knowledge.

- [FOSS Jobs](https://www.fossjobs.net/) — a specialist job source focused on free and open-source software. Start here for roles explicitly connected to FOSS.
- [OpenSSF Job Board](https://openssf.org/job-board/) — opportunities related to open-source software security, a useful specialism for developers and security professionals.
- [Linux Foundation Careers](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/about/careers) — roles at the Linux Foundation, which supports open-source projects and ecosystems.
- [Mozilla Careers](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/careers/listings/) — roles at Mozilla, including work connected to the open Internet and web technologies.
- [Wikimedia Foundation Careers](https://wikimediafoundation.org/about/jobs/) — opportunities supporting Wikipedia, Wikimedia projects and the free-knowledge ecosystem.
- [Open Technology Fund](https://www.opentech.fund/) — check its official site for roles and programmes related to Internet freedom and open technologies; the homepage is a starting point rather than a dedicated vacancy listing.

These organisations do not necessarily have relevant vacancies at all times. Use their official recruitment pages or announcements to confirm whether a suitable position exists.

## Paid contribution programmes and mentorships {#programmes}

Some routes into FOSS involve a defined contribution period or structured mentorship rather than conventional employment. Check the current programme rules, eligibility, application window and compensation before applying.

- [Outreachy](https://www.outreachy.org/) — paid, mentored internships contributing to open-source and other open projects. An internship does not guarantee later employment.
- [Google Summer of Code](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/) — a stipend-supported programme for contributing to open-source projects. Participation is not employment with Google.
- [LFX Mentorship](https://mentorship.lfx.linuxfoundation.org/) — mentorship opportunities connected with open-source projects. Compensation and terms depend on the particular programme.

Do not assume that a programme is paid simply because it is called a fellowship, internship or mentorship. Confirm the terms on the provider's own website.

## Internet freedom and public-interest technology {#public-interest}

The following organisations work on digital rights, Internet freedom, technology policy or public-interest research. Their opportunities may be occasional, and their general websites are not evidence that recruitment is currently open.

- [Internet Society - Careers](https://www.internetsociety.org/careers/) — roles connected to an open, globally connected and trustworthy Internet, including policy and community work.
- [ICANN - Careers](https://www.icann.org/en/careers) — work related to Internet identifiers, domain names and the coordination systems that help the Internet function.
- [Internet Governance Forum - Vacancies](https://www.intgovforum.org/en/vacancies) — check for roles and opportunities associated with the UN Internet Governance Forum.
- [Electronic Frontier Foundation - Jobs](https://www.eff.org/about/jobs) — civil liberties, privacy, security and freedom of expression online.
- [Access Now](https://www.accessnow.org/) — digital rights and human rights in the digital age; consult official announcements for opportunities.
- [Internet Freedom Foundation](https://internetfreedom.in/) — digital rights and Internet freedom work in India; check the official site for announcements.
- [SFLC.in](https://sflc.in/) — technology law, digital rights and policy work in India.
- [Digital Futures Lab - Careers](https://www.digitalfutureslab.in/careers) — research on technology and society, including AI governance and digital public infrastructure.

These sources are selected for their connection to the subject, not because they are steady sources of vacancies. The list is intentionally selective rather than a directory of every organisation in adjacent fields.

## Before applying {#before-applying}

- **Verify the original announcement.** Prefer the organisation's own vacancy page over a repost or search result.
- **Check eligibility and location.** "Remote" does not necessarily mean open worldwide. Check country restrictions, work authorisation, time zone and travel requirements.
- **Confirm the terms.** Look for the employment type, salary or stipend, duration, deadline and required documents. If these are not stated, do not guess.
- **Be alert to scams.** Verify the organisation's domain and recruiter identity. Be wary of requests for application fees, passwords, one-time passcodes or unnecessary identity documents.
- **Protect personal information.** Read the organisation's privacy notice before submitting sensitive information, and do not share financial credentials with an unverified contact.

The safest way to check a lead is to navigate from the organisation's official website and confirm the opportunity there. A link on this page is not a guarantee that a listing is current or legitimate.
