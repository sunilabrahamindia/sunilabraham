**Known Issues** are aspects of The Sunil Abraham Project (TSAP) that require improvement, attention, correction or modification, as well as known limitations and earlier decisions that are worth documenting for future reference.

This page serves as a gradually updated record of such issues. Some may require a fix or improvement, while others may need further attention, a modification, or simply documentation of the existing situation. Not every issue necessarily requires a change; in some cases, the current arrangement may be retained for practical reasons. The purpose is to keep these matters documented so that they can be reviewed and addressed where appropriate.

## List of Known Issues {#list}

*Known issues will be added to this list gradually as they are identified and documented. Each entry will describe the relevant issue, limitation or area requiring attention, along with any necessary context. The list will be updated as issues are reviewed, addressed or otherwise resolved.*
