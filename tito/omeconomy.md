**OmEconomy** is an idea being explored through an ongoing mind-mapping exercise about an economy in which contemplative repetition, or *japa*, is the only way to earn money. Individuals may choose any word or phrase for their japa; however, this particular exercise uses *Om* (ॐ) as its root word, with each genuinely contemplative repetition generating ₹1. The exercise imagines this single source of income operating within the present world, where existing goods and services remain available and the money earned can be spent on anything.

## The central idea

The exercise begins with an interest in contemplation and the cultivation of focus of mind. It explores what an economy might look like if this contemplative practice became the sole basis for earning money.

A person earns money by repeating a chosen word or phrase mentally. The repetition counts only when it is accompanied by contemplation and sustained attention. Merely repeating the word mechanically, while distracted or without focus, does not count.

*Om* is used for convenience in thinking through the idea. The underlying activity could involve any word or phrase; it does not have to be Om. For the purpose of this initial exploration, however, one qualifying repetition of Om generates ₹1.

## The sole source of income

The defining feature being explored is that there is only one route to earning money. There are no separate income-generating routes through employment, business, trade, investment or other economic activity. Those activities may continue to exist in the world, but they do not independently generate income. Money enters people's hands only through qualifying japa.

The illustrative rate makes the idea easy to follow: 100 qualifying repetitions generate ₹100, while 1,000 generate ₹1,000. These examples simply express the rule being explored.

## Spending in the existing world

The idea does not begin by imagining a different set of goods and services. It starts with the world as it exists now: its shops, businesses, factories, infrastructure, products and services. The change being explored concerns how people obtain money, not what is available to buy.

Money earned through japa can be spent on anything. It can pay for daily necessities such as food, housing and transport, as well as education, healthcare, entertainment, luxury goods and other available products or services. There is no restriction on spending based on how the money was earned.

## An ongoing mind map

These notes record the initial shape of the OmEconomy idea as it is being explored. They are not a finished economic theory, a formal proposal or a claim that such a system exists. The purpose at this stage is to lay out the central premise and its basic rules clearly, leaving room for further questions, connections and possibilities to emerge as the mind map develops.
