**OmEconomy** is an ongoing mind-mapping exercise exploring an imagined economy in which contemplative japa is the sole source of income. Individuals may choose any word or phrase for their japa. For this particular exercise, *Om* (ॐ) is the root word, and each genuinely contemplative repetition generates ₹1.

## Central idea

Money can be earned only through japa. A repetition counts when it is accompanied by contemplation and focus of mind; mechanical or distracted repetition does not count.

Employment, business, trade, investment and other economic activities may continue to exist, but they do not independently generate income. Money enters people's hands only through qualifying japa.

## Mathematical illustrations

**Rate:** 1 qualifying japa = ₹1  
**Illustrative pace:** 60 qualifying japa per minute  
**Basis:** 30-day month; weekly figures are calculated as 7/30 of the monthly target.

### Monthly target: ₹50,000

\[
\frac{\text{₹50,000}}{\text{₹1}} = 50{,}000\;\text{japa per month}
\]

| Period | Required japa (average) | Time at 60 japa/minute |
|:--|--:|--:|
| Month | 50,000 | 13 hr 53 min 20 sec |
| Week | ≈11,667 | ≈3 hr 14 min 27 sec |
| Day | ≈1,667 | ≈27 min 47 sec |

### Monthly target: ₹2,00,000

\[
\frac{\text{₹2,00,000}}{\text{₹1}} = 2{,}00{,}000\;\text{japa per month}
\]

| Period | Required japa (average) | Time at 60 japa/minute |
|:--|--:|--:|
| Month | 2,00,000 | 55 hr 33 min 20 sec |
| Week | ≈46,667 | ≈12 hr 57 min 47 sec |
| Day | ≈6,667 | ≈1 hr 51 min 7 sec |

**Calculation rules**

- Monthly japa = monthly income target ÷ ₹1
- Daily average = monthly japa ÷ 30
- Weekly average = monthly japa × 7 ÷ 30
- Time in minutes = japa ÷ 60

The daily and weekly figures are averages and are rounded to the nearest whole repetition or second. The monthly totals remain exact. The pace of 60 japa per minute is an illustration for calculation; only repetitions accompanied by contemplation and focus of mind qualify.

## Spending

The existing world of goods and services remains in place. People can spend the money earned through japa on anything available, including food, housing, transport, education, healthcare, entertainment and other products or services. The imagined change concerns how people earn money, not what they can buy.

## History and development

The exercise begins with an interest in contemplation and the cultivation of focus of mind. OmEconomy is being explored from that starting point through an evolving mind map. This page records its current ideas; further questions and connections can be added as the exercise develops.
