**OmEconomy** is a proposed economic model in which contemplative repetition, or *japa*, is the sole means of generating money. In its simplest form, each qualifying repetition of a designated word or phrase generates one unit of currency. The model uses *Om* (ॐ) as its representative japa word and assumes, for illustration, that each contemplative repetition earns one Indian rupee. Unlike conventional economies, in which income can arise from employment, business, trade, investment and other activities, OmEconomy has only one route to earning: focused contemplation.

## The basic principle

The central idea is that money is generated through the act of japa. A person earns currency by repeating a chosen word or phrase mentally, provided the repetition takes place with sustained attention and contemplation. Mechanical repetition or repetition performed without focus does not qualify under the model.

Although *Om* is used here for convenience, the underlying principle is not necessarily limited to that word. Another word or phrase could serve as the object of japa. The currency-generation rule remains the same: one qualifying repetition produces one unit of money.

## A single source of income

OmEconomy replaces the many routes through which people ordinarily earn money with one common mechanism. Employment, business profits, trading, investment returns and other activities do not independently generate income in this model. The only source of newly earned money is contemplative japa.

The example rate is deliberately simple: one qualifying repetition of Om generates ₹1. A person who completes 100 qualifying repetitions therefore earns ₹100; 1,000 repetitions generate ₹1,000. These figures illustrate the proposed rule rather than establish a limit on how much someone may earn.

## Spending and the existing economy

The model does not require a different set of goods and services. It can be imagined as being introduced into the present world, with its existing shops, businesses, factories, infrastructure, products and services. The fundamental change is to the source of people's income, not to the range of things they can buy.

Money earned through japa can be spent freely. It may be used for daily necessities such as food, housing and transport, or for education, healthcare, entertainment, luxury goods and any other available product or service. There is no separate category of spending tied to the way the money was earned.

## A thought experiment

OmEconomy is a thought experiment about what an economy might look like if contemplative activity were its only source of income. It does not describe an existing monetary system or a real-world payment arrangement. Its central proposition is the replacement of multiple income-generating routes with a single rule: money is earned through focused japa and spent through the ordinary economy.
