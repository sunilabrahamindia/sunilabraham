***Dalit Sahityam*** (Malayalam: ദലിത് സാഹിത്യം) is a Malayalam-language book by Paul Chirakkarod and [Athanasius Mathen Abraham Ayrookuzhiel](/amaa/), published in Tiruvalla in 1995. This book followed their earlier work [*Dalit Kavithakal: Oru Padanam*](/amaa/dalit-kavithakal/), published in 1992 with M. Sathyaprakasham.

The 1995 book is cited in a University of Glasgow doctoral thesis under the title *Dalit Sahityam: Oru Padanam*. The relevant discussion appears on PDF pages 218 and 224. The thesis gives the place of publication as Tiruvalla and the publisher as CSS.

## About the Book

*Dalit Sahityam* deals with Dalit literature and experience. The University of Glasgow doctoral thesis cites the book in its discussion of Dalit experience and oppression.

The discussion includes the experience of Dalits and forms of social oppression, as well as references to literary works and the representation of Dalit experience. The thesis provides an important independent scholarly reference to the 1995 book.
