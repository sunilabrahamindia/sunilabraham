**Editorial Source Audit: Fourth Stage** records the next phase of editorial work for *Essays on Dalits, Religion and Liberation II* by A. M. Abraham Ayrookuzhiel. It follows the work documented in the [first-stage Editorial Source Audit](/amaa/30-editorial-source-audit/), the [second-stage Editorial Source Audit](/amaa/30-editorial-source-audit-second-stage/) and the [third-stage Editorial Source Audit](/amaa/30-editorial-source-audit-third-stage/).

The earlier stages recorded the collection-wide capitalisation and italicisation review, comparisons with the original printed sources, heading consistency work and the development of editorial conventions. This fourth stage will take up the remaining work identified during those reviews, together with further source editing, checks and balances, style checks and other final editorial requirements.

The work will be recorded as it is undertaken. Each entry should distinguish the material examined, the issue identified, the decision taken, any source change made and the verification completed. An item must not be described as complete unless the relevant check has actually been carried out.

## Purpose and scope

This stage will provide a working record of:

- Outstanding matters carried forward from the first, second and third stages.
- Further source editing and corrections approved during the present review.
- Checks and balances across the essays and other parts of the book, including consistency, completeness and internal cross-checks.
- Further style checks and the application of the collection's established editorial conventions.
- Editorial and production-related work that affects the readiness of the book.
- Verification of changes and a record of matters that remain unresolved.

The scope is not limited to the nine essays. Where relevant, it may include the Foreword, Editor's Note, About the Author, credit page, notes, references and other parts of the book. Any work on the print-production system will be identified separately from changes to the manuscript source.

## Relationship with the earlier stages

The earlier audit pages remain the records of the work completed and decisions made in their respective stages. This fourth-stage page does not replace or retrospectively rewrite those records.

## Working method

For each activity, record, where applicable:

1. The essay, section, production file or other material examined.
2. The check or editorial question undertaken.
3. The finding, including any discrepancy or uncertainty.
4. The decision and its rationale.
5. The source or production change made, if authorised.
6. The verification performed and its result.
7. Any outstanding action.

Source corrections should be made only after the relevant passage has been examined in context. Avoid broad or automatic changes where individual judgement is required. Preserve the author's meaning, wording and argument unless a specific correction has been established and approved.

**Review of the earlier audit records**

The first-, second- and third-stage audit files were read from the repository to establish the current editorial record, the decisions already made and the matters that may require further attention. This fourth stage is intended to continue from those records, not repeat completed work.

**Credit page redesign and production credits**

The EDRL II print credit page was revised in `ebook/edrl-ii/print/build.py`. The work included removing the "Printed in India" line and its empty grid cell, changing the printer location to "National Print Press, India", simplifying and left-aligning the title and credits, retaining the source-attribution and ISBN/price information, and presenting the e-book address as a clickable link.

The credit grid was also updated to identify the Editor, Typesetting and Print Coordination roles separately. The technical production paragraph was restored under the heading "PRODUCTION AND TYPESETTING", recording the use of Python, Pandoc, Typst, GitHub and GitHub Actions.

The source edits and subsequent syntax corrections were committed. The latest email-escaping correction is recorded in commit `8e9f219e372e0d7d13096e61c4f7cee424fbfa47`. The preceding print attempts exposed a bracket error and an email-escaping error; the latest correction requires a successful workflow run and visual PDF inspection before the redesigned page can be marked as fully verified.

**Status:** Credit-page source revision recorded. Final print-build and visual verification remain to be confirmed.


### 18 November 2026 — Article typography and poem lineation

**Spacing before punctuation: Essays 5–9**

A focused check was carried out for unwanted spaces immediately before full stops, commas, semicolons, colons, question marks and exclamation marks in the article/full-text sections.

- **Essay 8 — Dalit Liberation—Some Reflections on Their Ideological Predicament:** 13 spacing instances were corrected within the full-text section, including the heading "Sony !". The source was re-fetched and checked; no instances remained in the checked section.
- **Essay 9 — The Ideological Nature of the Emerging Dalit Consciousness:** 4 spacing instances were corrected within the full-text section. The source was re-fetched and checked; no instances remained in the checked section.
- **Essay 7 — Dalit Theology: A Movement of Counter-Culture:** No instances were found in the article body. No punctuation-spacing changes were made.
- **Essay 6 — Religion: A Way of Salvation or an Ideology of Oppression?:** No instances were found in the article body. No punctuation-spacing changes were made.
- **Essay 5 — Religions and the Aspirations of the People:** No instances were found in the Full text section. No punctuation-spacing changes were made.

These were limited typographic-normalisation checks. They do not constitute a complete proofread of the essays or a verification of other sections such as publication details and notes.

**Essay 7 — lineation of the Sivavakkiyar passage**

The poem passage beginning "Who is an untouchable woman and who is a Brahman woman?" was reformatted from seven short lines into four lines. The first three lines now end at the question marks, and the concluding sentence remains on the fourth line. The existing `<div class="poem">` wrapper, wording, punctuation and footnote markup were retained. No other content in the essay was changed.

The source change was committed as `80796f66a2455e756f133956f5e5baf27cdd0a71`.

A separate four-line passage beginning "Not by birth does one become an outcaste" was reviewed for capitalisation. The decision was to retain the existing mixed capitalisation rather than capitalise every line: the lowercase beginnings of the second and third lines preserve the sentence's continuity. No change was made to that passage.

**Status:** The two punctuation-spacing corrections and the Essay 7 lineation change were committed. The relevant source sections were checked after the punctuation corrections; no print build or PDF layout verification was undertaken for the poem change.
