***Dalit Kavithakal: Oru Padanam*** (Malayalam: *ദലിത് കവിതകള്‍ ഒരു പഠനം*; *Dalit Poems: A Study*) is a Malayalam-language book by Paul Chirakkarodu, M. Sathyaprakasham and [Athanasius Mathen Abraham Ayrookuzhiel](https://sunilabraham.in/amaa/), published in Tiruvalla by Christian Literature Society (CLS) in 1992.

## Bibliographic Details

- **Title:** *Dalit Kavithakal: Oru Padanam*
- **Malayalam title:** *ദലിത് കവിതകള്‍ ഒരു പഠനം*
- **English title:** *Dalit Poems: A Study*
- **Authors:** Paul Chirakkarodu, M. Sathyaprakasham and Athanasius Mathen Abraham Ayrookuzhiel
- **Language:** Malayalam
- **Publisher:** Christian Literature Society (CLS)
- **Place of publication:** Tiruvalla
- **Year:** 1992

## Background

*Dalit Kavithakal: Oru Padanam* is cited in scholarly literature concerned with Dalit theology, caste, religion and identity in India. An English-language doctoral thesis from the University of Glasgow refers to the work at several points, including discussions associated with pages 56 and 191–192. The book is also cited in an English-language discussion of Dalit theology under the English title *Dalit Poems: A Study*, confirming its publication in Tiruvalla in 1992.

The book has also been included in university-level Malayalam literature curricula. Sree Sankaracharya University of Sanskrit lists *ദലിത് കവിതകള്‍ ഒരു പഠനം* among works prescribed for the study of Malayalam literature and identifies Christian Literature Society of Tiruvalla as its publisher.

These references place the book within the development and academic study of Dalit literature and thought in Malayalam.
