## Visual description

This wordless comic follows Ti in a peaceful, leafy forest. He stands facing forward among tall trees, and his expression gradually changes into a fuller, more contented smile. Sunlight grows brighter and the patches of visible sky become increasingly blue. Leaves and greenery slowly spread across Ti as his body takes on the form of a tree. Magical light and subtle cosmic imagery emerge in the later panels, becoming brighter and more expansive in the final panel, while the forest remains present throughout. The sequence suggests a quiet merging with nature and a sense of liberation. There are no dialogue, captions or written words within the panels.
