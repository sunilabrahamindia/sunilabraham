## Visual description

A wordless TSPA comic arranged as six vertical panels. Ti sits beside a large lake or pond in a bright natural landscape. In the earlier panels, a dense, colourful and chaotic mass of paper-like fragments, thought bubbles and ribbon-like forms surrounds the area above and around his head, visually representing mental chatter. Ti gathers and throws these forms into the water, where they create ripples and splashes. As the sequence progresses, the chaotic material becomes less dense and drifts away, while the lake and sky become more open and peaceful. In the final panel, only a small amount of the visual chatter remains and Ti sits quietly beside the calm water with a relaxed, blissful expression and open eyes.

The paper-like fragments and bubbles are intentionally abstract and unreadable rather than written in any particular language. The artwork contains no dialogue, captions or other written words within the comic panels.
