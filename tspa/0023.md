## Transcript

**Panel 1 - Ti:** "Thinking..."

**Panel 2 - Mi:** "Thinking again? What are you thinking about?"

**Panel 3 - Ti:** "I'm thinking about many things. About silence, about being quiet, about the absence of sound and chaos."

**Panel 4:** Ti and Mi look at each other in silence.

**Panel 5 - Ti:** "Words and thoughts weigh heavily. They burden my mind, burden my very existence. I'm thinking..."

**Panel 6 - Mi:** "So, has all this thinking achieved anything? Is anything moving forward?"

**Panel 7 - Ti:** "The day has passed in thinking, thinking..."

## Visual details

The seven-panel comic takes place on a leafy porch, with Ti in his familiar red T-shirt and Mi, the white-and-orange cat, sitting beside him. Ti starts by admitting that he is thinking, and Mi asks what is on his mind. Ti describes his thoughts about silence, quietness, and the absence of sound and chaos. The fourth panel gives the conversation a wordless pause: Ti and Mi look at each other, with a small butterfly crossing the garden. Ti then describes the weight of words and thoughts, saying that they burden both his mind and his existence. Mi asks whether all this thinking has led to any progress. In the final panel, the pair laugh gently at the passing day and Ti's repeated thinking. Their eyes remain open as they laugh.
