## Transcript

**Frame 1:** Mi searches the cluttered study room.

**Frame 2 - Mi:** "কই গো তুমি, টি?"

**Frame 3:** Mi continues searching different parts of the room.

**Frame 4 - Mi:** "টি কোথায় গেল?"

**Frame 5:** Mi continues the search around the study room.

**Frame 6 - Mi:** "বেশ কিছুদিন হল দেখছি না। কোথায় যে গেল টি?"

## English translation

**Frame 1:** Mi searches the cluttered study room.

**Frame 2 - Mi:** "Where are you, Ti?"

**Frame 3:** Mi continues searching different parts of the room.

**Frame 4 - Mi:** "Where has Ti gone?"

**Frame 5:** Mi continues the search around the study room.

**Frame 6 - Mi:** "I haven't seen Ti for quite some time. Where could Ti have gone?"

## Visual description

In a cluttered study room filled with books, papers, a desktop computer, a desk lamp, stationery and other everyday objects, Mi searches for Ti. Mi looks around the room, checks different areas and continues the search, growing increasingly puzzled at Ti's absence. Ti does not appear in the comic.
