## Visual description

A wordless TSPA comic arranged as a six-panel vertical sequence. Ti faces forward in every panel beneath a large full moon and a deep blue-to-black night sky. His gaze becomes progressively more focused as the sequence advances. His eyes retain human-like proportions, while the whites gradually develop a reddish tint. At the same time, his expression moves from neutral to an increasingly clear and warm smile. The moon and surrounding sky undergo a dramatic visual transition from cool blue and black tones towards intense warm red and orange tones, while the silhouetted landscape and clouds reinforce the changing atmosphere.

The artwork contains no dialogue, captions or other written words within the comic panels.
