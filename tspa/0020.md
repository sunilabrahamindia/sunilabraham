## Transcript

**Panel 1 - Ti:** "I will start with Ashtavakra Gita. This looks amazing!"

**Panel 2 - Ti:** "Next I must read The Gospel of Sri Ramakrishna. I have heard so much about it."

**Panel 3 - Ti:** "This one too! Seven Years in Tibet. What a journey this must be! I must read this."

**Panel 4 - Ti:** "I really want to read The Brothers Karamazov. A must read!"

**Panel 5 - Ti:** "And of course, Superintelligence. I should read this to understand AI better."

**Panel 6 - Ti:** "Life 3.0 is next on my list. So many important ideas here."

**Panel 7 - Ti:** "This too! The Coming Wave. So many important questions about our future."

**Panel 8 - Ti:** "So many books... I plan to read every now and then. But it looks like I miss, I fail to read them."

## Visual details

The eight-panel comic shows Ti and Mi in a warm, book-filled home library. Across the first seven panels, Ti enthusiastically picks up different books he intends to read, including books on spirituality, religion, travel, classic literature and artificial intelligence. More books accumulate around him as his reading list grows. In the final panel, the scene has shifted to a later time, with a large pile of books and Ti reflecting on how he keeps planning to read them but does not manage to get around to them. Mi remains present and awake in the scenes.
