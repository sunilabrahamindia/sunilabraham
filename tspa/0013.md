## Transcript

**Frame 1 - To:** "I am To. An alter ego. When Ti sleeps, I am awake."

**Frame 2 - To:** "When Ti sleeps, I see the doubts, the uncomfortable questions, and the harder truths."

**Frame 3 - To:** "I'm not Ti's opposite. I am another side of the same Ti. Different moods. Different lenses. Same person."

**Frame 4 - Mo:** "I am Mo. An alter ego. When Mi sleeps, I am awake."

**Frame 5 - Mo:** "When Mi sleeps, I notice what others might miss. I am more cautious, sometimes cynical, but I care just as much."

**Frame 6 - Mo:** "I'm not Mi's opposite. I am another side of the same Mi. Different moods. Different lenses. Same cat."

**Frame 7 - Ti:** "Love you."

**Frame 7 - To:** "**** you."

**Frame 8 - Mi:** "I'll pray for you."

**Frame 8 - Mo:** "I'll see you in hell."

**Frame 9:** Ti and To are together. The closing text says: "We are Ti and To. Together, we are Tito. The curious and the critical. Both are me."

**Frame 10:** Mi and Mo are together. The closing text says: "We are Mi and Mo. Together, we are Mimo. The playful and the cautious. Both are me."

## Visual details

The comic introduces To as Ti's alter ego and Mo as Mi's alter ego. Ti is shown sleeping while To is awake, and Mi is shown sleeping while Mo is awake. To and Mo represent different moods and lenses of the same underlying characters rather than simple opposites. The final frames bring Ti and To together as Tito, and Mi and Mo together as Mimo.
