## Transcript

**Panel 1 - Man:** "You are very helpful. Thank you for your cooperation."

**Panel 2 - People:** "He has done a lot of volunteer work." / "We are thankful to him." / "He is always ready to help."

**Panel 3 - Woman:** "Thanks for your input."

**Panel 4 - Man:** "Thanks for all your help. We really appreciate it."

**Panel 5 - Ti (thinking):** "I've received so many thanks! Wow!"

**Panel 6 - Ti:** "My mother is in ICU. I don't have much money, but I've received a lot of thanks. Can I pay in thanks?"

**Panel 6 - Receptionist:** "I'm sorry, we can't accept thanks as payment. You need to pay the actual hospital bills in actual money."

**Panel 7 - Ti:** "I have to pay the bill. I've collected a lot of thanks. Can I pay the bill with thanks?"

**Panel 7 - Shopkeeper:** "Thanks cannot be used here. You have to pay in actual money for your purchase."

**Panel 8 - Ti:** "I want to buy a 2 BHK house. I've got a lot of thanks. Can I pay with thanks?"

**Panel 8 - Estate agent:** "I appreciate your efforts, but thanks is not a form of payment. You need to pay the actual price in money."

**Panel 9 - Ti (thinking):** "I've received so many thanks... But thanks alone cannot solve real problems."

## Visual details

The comic begins outdoors, where Ti is praised by a man and by several people for being helpful and doing volunteer work. A woman thanks him for his input, and another man appreciates his help. Ti feels delighted by the many expressions of gratitude. He then tries to use those thanks to pay for his mother's ICU care, groceries and a 2 BHK home. Each person he asks gives a different response, while making the same point: gratitude is meaningful, but it cannot replace money when paying for essential goods and services. In the final wide panel, Ti sits sadly on the steps beside Mi, thinking about the limits of thanks when real problems need practical solutions.
