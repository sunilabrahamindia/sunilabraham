## Visual description

A wordless TSPA comic arranged as a visual composition rather than a linear sequence. A large central circular scene shows Ti facing forward beneath a full moon and a dramatic night sky. Around it are smaller circular scenes showing Ti with different facial expressions, including happiness, surprise, puzzlement, sadness, irritation, calmness and thoughtfulness. The surrounding circles use varied visual treatments, including colour, muted tones and monochrome backgrounds.

The artwork contains no dialogue, captions or other written words within the comic panels. The visual arrangement and Ti's changing expressions are intended to carry the idea.
