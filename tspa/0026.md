## Visual description

This wordless comic follows Ti and Cha through a sequence of interconnected, jigsaw-shaped scenes. The pair move through surreal environments built from layered geometric forms, textured colour fields, fragments of binary digits, luminous pathways and shifting architectural shapes. Each panel uses a distinct background palette, ranging from warm yellows and oranges to cool blues and greens, deep purples, and cosmic colours. Ti and Cha's actions and viewpoints vary across the panels: they leap, walk together, pause, help each other, run, rest beneath a starry sky and look towards a distant horizon. The changing environments and their shared movement suggest an ongoing journey of companionship, mutual influence and discovery. There are no dialogue, captions or written words within the panels.
