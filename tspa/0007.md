## Transcript

**Frame 1 - Ti:** "Alright, this is pretty late at night. I'll sleep."

**Frame 2 - Ti:** "Oh, I forgot to put an alarm for 6 am tomorrow. Where is my phone?"

**Frame 3:** Ti watches reels on his phone.

**Frame 4:** Ti continues using social media on his phone while Mi looks on.

**Frame 5:** Ti continues using his phone late into the night while Mi looks increasingly unimpressed.

**Frame 6 - Ti:** "It is already dawn. I need to sleep."

**Frame 7 - Ti:** "Oh, I forgot to put an alarm. Where is my phone?"

**Frame 8 - Mi:** "He actually took the phone at 11 pm for the exact same thing — putting an alarm."

**Visual details:** The eight panels follow Ti and Mi in a bedroom from 11:00 pm through dawn. The lighting gradually changes from deep night to sunrise. Ti's late-night phone use becomes increasingly prolonged, while Mi's reactions become increasingly unimpressed. In the final panel, Ti is asleep and Mi is awake, with a slightly brighter dawn sky.
