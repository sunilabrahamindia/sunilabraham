## Transcript

**Panel 1:** A farmer and Cha look across a drought-stricken field beneath the hot sun.

**Panel 2 - Farmer:** "Please let it rain. Our fields need water!"

**Panel 3 - Cha:** "And please give me a lot of water for cooling my data centre!"

**Panel 4:** The farmer looks at Cha with surprise and disbelief, while Cha continues praying.

## Visual description

The four-panel comic takes place in a dry agricultural field in India. Under a harsh sun, cracked earth and withered crops show the effects of drought. The farmer folds his hands and asks for rain to save the fields. Cha, a white conversational robot, also folds its hands, but asks for plenty of water to cool its data centre. In the final panel, the farmer turns towards Cha with a startled, disbelieving look. The punchline contrasts the farmer's urgent need for water with the water demands of data-centre cooling.
