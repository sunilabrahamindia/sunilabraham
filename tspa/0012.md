## Transcript

**Frame 1:** A speaker is presenting at a conference on open source software. Ti and Mi are among the audience.

**Frame 2 - Speaker:** "We must use open source."

**Frame 3 - Speaker:** "We must encourage open source."

**Frame 4 - Speaker:** "Open source is the way forward!"

**Frame 5:** Ti and Mi look at each other with a doubtful expression.

**Frame 6 - Mi:** "Looks like the speaker is preaching, but not practising."

## Visual details

The comic takes place at a conference. The speaker is presenting about open source software while visibly using a proprietary-looking laptop and smartphone with stylised, non-brand-specific logos. Ti and Mi are seated in the audience. In the final frame, Mi independently observes the contradiction and delivers the punchline while both Ti and Mi laugh.
