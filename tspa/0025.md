## Visual description

This silent comic shows Ti sitting cross-legged with a mala in his hand. Across eight panels, he counts on his fingers, first with his right hand and then with his left, while maintaining a calm, attentive expression. The panels have vivid, varied backgrounds and irregular shapes outlined with colourful, pearl-like dotted borders. The repeated gestures and mala evoke practices of rhythmic counting, mantra repetition, and sustained attention, without explaining the idea in words. There are no captions, dialogue or numbers inside the panels.
