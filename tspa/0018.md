## Visual description

A wordless TSPA comic arranged in six smaller oval panels followed by one large concluding panel. The first three panels show Ti creating in warm morning or daylight settings: carefully building a brick house, tending a garden and watering plants, and painting a landscape. The corresponding three panels show To destroying those creations under darker night skies: breaking the house with a hammer, uprooting the garden, and damaging the painting. The final large panel brings Ti and To together in a single split image, with Ti's side associated with daylight and creation and To's side associated with night and destruction.

The artwork contains no dialogue, captions or other written words within the comic panels. The contrasting imagery presents construction and destruction as two sides of the same existence.
