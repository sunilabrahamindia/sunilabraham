<h2>Comic transcript</h2>

<p><strong>Scene:</strong> At night, Ti sits on a grassy hillside beside Mi, looking towards a city skyline under a crescent moon and a starry sky.</p>

<p><strong>Ti:</strong> "Oh, one new cartoon strip, and they call it TSPA!!"</p>

<p><strong>Ti:</strong> "I'll wait and see what it aims to convey and capture - Technology, life, the singularity... or will it just become a satire or commentary strip?"</p>

<p><strong>Visual details:</strong> Ti is seated beside Mi, a cat. Trees frame the left side of the scene, while the distant city has illuminated windows beneath the night sky.</p>
