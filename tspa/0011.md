## English translation

**Frame 1 - Ti:** "I heard a wonderful song, you know?"

**Frame 1 - Mi:** "Oh, really? Sing it, sing it."

**Frame 2 - Ti:** "If the river were liquor..."

**Frame 2 - Mi:** listening

**Frame 3 - Ti:** "... I would drink my fill while drunk..."

**Frame 3 - Mi:** listening

**Frame 4:** Ti and Mi are silent, in a dramatic pause.

**Frame 5 - Ti:** "... and the snack would be sand, what have you done, Ma Kali..."

**Frame 5 - Mi:** listening

**Frame 6 - Ti:** "Oh elder brother, oh elder sister, the bats play the madal drum...."

**Frame 6 - Mi:** listening

**Frame 7:** Ti and Mi are silent, in a dramatic pause.

**Frame 8 - Mi:** "Wonderful song. But why did you suddenly start singing this song?"

**Frame 9 - Ti:** "I'm thinking, if there were an opportunity to use AI tools in abundance in the same way, how much fun that would be. I could learn and find out so many things easily."

**Frame 10:** Ti and Mi laugh together.

**Visual details:** Ti and Mi sit beside a riverside surrounded by lush greenery. Ti sings enthusiastically while Mi listens, with dramatic silent pauses in Frames 4 and 7. The final conversation leads to Ti reflecting on how abundant access to AI tools could make it easier to learn and discover many things, ending with both laughing together.
