## Transcript

**Panel 1 - Ti:** "So, let us start the meeting."

**Panel 2 - Ti:** "Let's begin with silence. Silence is more powerful than words."

**Panels 3-7:** The team remains deliberately silent, attentive and contemplative.

**Panel 8 - Ti:** "All right. Now let's begin the meeting."

## Visual details

The eight-panel comic takes place in a formal corporate boardroom. Ti chairs the meeting at the centre of a conference table, surrounded by colleagues in professional business attire. After Ti proposes beginning with silence, the next five panels show the team sharing a deliberate pause. Their eyes remain open; subtle changes in expression, posture and eye contact convey attention and reflection rather than confusion or sleepiness. The oval panels and gradual transition from warm, oil-like colours to cooler, acrylic-like daylight reinforce the shift from the bustle of a typical meeting to a calmer, more attentive atmosphere. The final line gently punctures the premise: only after the period of silence does Ti announce that the meeting can begin.
